# RetailPulse - 5 Minute Demo Script

**0:00-0:30** - Introduce RetailPulse and the business question: understand customer value, churn risk, revenue trajectory and retention.

**0:30-1:15** - Overview: show revenue, customer count, order count, AOV, repeat rate and monthly trend.

**1:15-2:00** - Segments: explain RFM, show elbow + silhouette, open the four business profiles and compare their RFM behavior.

**2:00-3:00** - Churn: explain the temporal churn label, show held-out AUC, precision/recall, ROC curve and SHAP beeswarm/waterfall.

**3:00-3:45** - Forecast: show SARIMA backtest MAPE and the three-month forward forecast with 95% intervals.

**3:45-4:30** - Cohorts: show the retention heatmap, M1/M6/M12 retention and cohort AOV.

**4:30-5:00** - Close with two or three evidence-based business actions and mention reproducibility, serialized models and MLflow tracking.
